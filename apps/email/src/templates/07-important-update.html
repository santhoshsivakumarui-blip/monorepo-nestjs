Important update

A change to how long we keep your vitals

From 1 November 2026 we keep raw vital readings for 24 months, then aggregate them into daily summaries and delete the per-minute series.

What this means for you

Your trends Unchanged — daily summaries go back indefinitely
Per-minute history Kept for 24 months
Prescriptions and scans Kept indefinitely
Your hospital’s records Unaffected — they keep their own copy

We made this change because two years of per-minute readings is roughly 50 million data points per person, and holding it forever is a risk to you rather than a service to you. Daily summaries answer every question the trends screen asks.

Want everything? Export your full raw history at any time from Settings → Download my data. We will keep sending you a link whenever you ask.
